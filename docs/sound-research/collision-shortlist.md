**Brief:** glass + crystal + physical impact. A small glass object hitting
another, crystal pieces touching, a glass bead on a hard surface, a delicate
glass/ceramic impact, a crystalline "tink" or "clink" — tactile, precise,
slightly sophisticated. **Not** a cartoon boing, a generic game hit, a metal
clang, a gunshot, an explosion, a harsh notification, or a childish game sound.

**What it represents:** two carbon atoms physically colliding → impact →
molecular bonding begins. It must read as *physical contact* **and**
*scientific/crystalline material*.

**Nobody has listened to these.** Every character description is inferred from
the asset page's title, description, tags, duration, sample rate and bit depth.
That is exactly why you are auditioning them.

**Licence status:** all 12 are cleared for commercial use — 11 × CC0, 1 ×
Pixabay Content License. None requires attribution.

**Audition links:** the "Preview" URLs below are Freesound's public CDN previews
(lossy MP3) and play straight in a browser with no login. The full-quality
originals need a free Freesound account; BigSoundBank and Pixabay download
directly.

## Rejected

### On provenance — the important one
**snd_fragment_retrieve.wav** — uploaded by *rocker12523*
(https://freesound.org/people/rocker12523/sounds/678497/) and again by *gristi*
(https://freesound.org/people/gristi/sounds/562196/), both tagged CC0, 0.272s.

On character this was **the single best match in the entire search** — the
uploader's own description reads "A crystal/glass sound of a resonant
collision". **Rejected anyway.** The filename uses the `snd_` GameMaker
asset-naming convention, and two unrelated accounts uploaded a file with the
identical internal asset name — strongly consistent with a rip from a
commercial game rather than an original recording. A CC0 tag applied by someone
who did not create the sound confers nothing. Origin could not be confirmed, so
it is uncertain provenance, which the brief says to reject. **Do not use it.**

### On technical quality
**clink on glass, dull** (freemaster2, CC0, 0.125s) — 8kHz mono, 764 bytes. The
high frequencies that carry the "glass" character do not exist in an 8kHz file.

### On character
Metal/`game`-tagged hits (Pickaxe2) · synthesised and explicitly `Cartoon`/
`Childish` (Classic Glass Hit-C6) · synthetic magic/UI (Crystal Shape 1, kwartz,
iceSpell, Fantasy_ui_Button_5) · a notification sound (Triple_Ping) · `metal`/
`clang` tagged (Placing something on a glass plate 2) · crystal singing bowls —
long sustained tones, not impacts · Pixabay Glass Knock 1 — reads as a knock on
a large pane, wrong scale · multi-hit sequences and ambiences (Tap_Tap_Tap,
Glass Clink Pack, bottles clinking, marbles in a bowl, ice cube in glass) ·
glass *breaking* results — wrong event entirely.

### Not rejected, but redirected
All Pixabay results credited to **freesound_community** are Freesound
re-uploads, including the wine-glass set. Use the Freesound originals, where the
per-sound licence is authoritative.

**No candidate was rejected for CC BY-NC** — every Freesound search ran with the
CC0 facet applied and each licence was then re-confirmed on the individual asset
page.
