# Atom collision and bond — candidates

**Engine events:** `ATOM_COLLISION`, then `BOND_CREATED`.
**Wanted feel:** impact → reaction → connection. Physical impact, scientific/tactile, satisfying contact, subtle energy, molecular connection.
**Excluded by brief:** explosions, gunshots, weapons, harsh arcade hits, cartoon BOOM, childish buzzers, franchise audio.

This is the highest-priority sound in the product and the one with the most
structure available to it — see "The two-part collision" at the end.

Nobody listened to these; character notes are inferred from page metadata.

## The two-part collision

The engine gives this sound more structure than the brief assumed. Verified
against the code:

```
t=0      ATOM_COLLISION   → contact sound, attack at sample 0, ≤260ms
t=+260   BOND_CREATED     → connection sound, ONLY if the bond formed
t=+460   bond complete    → nothing. Silence is the resolution.
```

**Three things this structure buys, all free:**

1. **It teaches without a failure buzzer.** `ATOM_COLLISION` carries
   `bonded: false` when the paper hits the wrong carbon group (`engine.ts:483`)
   or a hydrogen bounces off (`engine.ts:722`). A miss gets contact and *no
   resolution* — an unanswered question. A hit gets contact then connection.
   The player learns the difference before they can articulate it, and nothing
   ever scolds them.
2. **It separates cause from effect**, which is exactly what the animation spec
   builds four extra frames (E6.1–E6.3) to protect. One merged sound would undo
   that work.
3. **It can encode bond order.** `BOND_CREATED` carries `order` (1/2/3). Map it
   to pitch — single at root, double +7 semitones, triple +12 — or stack layers
   at 40ms offsets. A triple bond should *sound* denser than a single. That is
   free chemistry pedagogy, and it is ready for alkene/alkyne before either is
   built.

**Timing caveat, verified in the code — the two paths differ:**

- **Carbon collision** (paper → pair): `ATOM_COLLISION` is emitted at contact in
  `strikeGroup` (`engine.ts:511`) and `BOND_CREATED` 260ms later from
  `stepImpact` (`engine.ts:541`), in a different tick. **The gap is real; a
  direct subscriber gets the right timing for free.**
- **Hydrogen bond** (`resolveContact`, `engine.ts:713-714`): both are emitted
  **back-to-back in the same synchronous tick.** A naive subscriber would fire
  both sounds at 0ms and the two-part design would collapse into mush here.

So the audio layer should **schedule the connection sound off `ATOM_COLLISION`
using `bondStartMs()` from `carbonImpact.ts`**, rather than trusting
`BOND_CREATED` timing uniformly. Use `AudioContext.currentTime + 0.26` with Web
Audio scheduling, not `setTimeout`, or the gap will jitter under load.

**Chained collisions.** `ATOM_COLLISION` carries `chainDepth`, and several can
fire in quick succession from one throw. Three guards: round-robin at least
three contact variants; cap collision voices at four with oldest-first
stealing; detune by −1 semitone per `chainDepth` so a chain reads as a
descending cascade rather than a stutter.
